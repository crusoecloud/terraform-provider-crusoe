```release-note:bug
You can now move a `crusoe_compute_instance` to another InfiniBand partition in place by changing `transport_partition_id` or `ib_partition_id` in `host_channel_adapters`, instead of the apply failing with "Provider produced inconsistent result after apply". The VM is stopped for the change and started again if it was running, and a plan now rejects setting `ib_partition_id` and `transport_partition_id` to different partitions. See [Managing InfiniBand networking](https://docs.crusoe.ai/networking/infiniband/managing-infiniband-networks/).
```
